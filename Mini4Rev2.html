EMPTY SHELL

Game By CC ARTS, Review By Kachi-Kachi

Game tags: Action Roguelike, Shooter, Top-Down, Roguelite, Horror

TRAILER

INFO

EMPTY SHELL is a game that released in Oct 16, 2023 on PC, Nintendo Switch, Xbox 1 / Series X/S, PS 4/5 by CC ARTS and published by Hyperstrange. You play as group of people that are known as volunteer and you and the many after you are thown into an secret facility for a "recovery operation".

REVIEW

GAMEPLAY Fine and refined the various weapons, gadgets and items you use all are usable in there own ways. The enemy's you can face is can range form zombies to large sentient hands and many other horrors. EMPTY SHELL also has puzzles these were overall fun with one were you had to navigate an computer terminal mainly which was insanely immersive. The survival aspicts are done well the need to conserve your ammo, tools hp items. This all wrap up in a rougelike is nice. ARTSTYLE Dead simple and I love it. Its pixleated black and white with very rare uses of color. The atomosphere and astitc is this games strongest part if you like semi-science fiction coldwar look you will love this game's art. You trully feel alone and on your own acord. SOUND This game has no music, so the sound of EMPTY SHELL is disconeted feeling. Like your hearing form a recording which i feel works well for a top down camera view. Other than that the sound of the game is not much to white home about. STORY The Id say the largest part of EMPTY SHELL and the part I sould have payed more attion to since form what I got form it was not much but capivating. This is not realy a falt of the game and more of me gosing over lore text. If you are planing to get the game I very recomed you to look and read the lore of this game.
