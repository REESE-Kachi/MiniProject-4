Trepang2

Game By Trepang Studios, Review By Kachi-Kachi

Game tags: Shooter, Singleplayer, Horror ,FPS ,Gore, Violent

TRAILER

INFO

Trepang is a game that released in Jun 21, 2023 on PC, Xbox Series X/S and PS5 by Trepang Studios and published by Team17. You play as an near unstoppable super solder tasked to destroy the institution that took what freedom you had.

REVIEW

GAMEPLAY|Fantastic Trepang has this weighted but smooth movement and mixing this with the gun play and feel of them is great. The weapons you have 8 of them +3 if you buy the Bladekisser DLC, these all have unique attachments to find in the main missions. The missions are all memorable in there own unique way with all of them having an theme. The emery's you face are very fun to kill, them being model off of the F.E.A.R ai which mind you has one of the best shooter ai to date Trepang to equal it. Trepang's Gun play, map design, and ai make it a fantastic experience. ARTSTYLE|The art direction is well done overall with select set pieces looking fantastic. The art style is modern futuristic with other styles mix in for flavor. The graphics are realistic with enough fidelity to look good for years on. MUSIC|Dam good music tracks like Horizon Hail Mary, Patty, Thermonuclear and Cave Party are on my liked songs. The creator Brandon McKagan the creator of the Trepang OST did the great job. All songs fit in there environments and moods plus overall the OST has great range. STORYThere's two way of looking at the story what's given to you at face value and reading the lore documents and images. I did the former and even with the extra story the one that's given to you is still fun to go though. But if yo do find and look and read everything the story told with the extra info is fantastic. My only grip with it is the fact it is very miss able though I don't really know how to fix that with messing with the pacing.
