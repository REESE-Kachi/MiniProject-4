Delta Particles

Game By Yuri Epifantsev, Review By Kachi-Kachi

Game tags: Acton, Indie, FPS, Mod, ShooterFirst-Person, 3D

TRAILER

INFO

Delta Particles is a mod that released in Dec 20, 2024 on Steam by and published by Yuri Epifantsev. You play as Nick Farrell a technical engineer of complex "Delta Base" and was caught in wrong place at the wrong time.

REVIEW

GAMEPLAY Its half-Life so the gun play even for the new wepons are good. Along with the mod's credit it has good Level design and keeps it self at a good pace. The enemy ai is you guess is also good since its built on half-Life. So overall if you like half-Life's gameplay you will like this. ARTSTYLE Following the ternd of thsi being a mod Delta Particals looks like half-Life which half-Life's grafics are dated but still hold up. The mod's new textue work and new model also look right at home for a half-Life expantion. MUSIC Now unlike half-Life Delta Particles uses mostly atmospheric tracks. None of the tracks realy stood out which can be a good thing or bad. STORY The story is similar to the half-Life expanions. Delta Particles story is told in the same way as the half-Life games are told environmental storytelling and player controled story scenes. Overall the story is simple but its works well for what it is.
